# When intelligence gets cheap

What remains scarce?

Production, ownership and ordinary life.

A useful answer gets cheaper. A home, a power connection or a trustworthy decision may remain difficult to obtain.

As machines take on more cognitive work, what becomes possible, what remains limiting, and who gets to use the gain?
