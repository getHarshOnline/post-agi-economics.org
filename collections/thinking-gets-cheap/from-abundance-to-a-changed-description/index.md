Title: From abundance to a changed description

[Read HTML](https://post-agi-economics.org/collections/thinking-gets-cheap/from-abundance-to-a-changed-description/)

What does an economic comparison hold fixed? A production technology can stay the same while the price, residual income and recipient change, and expenditure can stay the same while two purchases cease to fit together. A claim that cognition becomes abundant raises three questions in order. What does an existing production model predict? Who receives the resulting saving? And when does the description need to change to explain a consequence it previously left out?

The first article derives a small production example and shows why ownership matters even when total output is unchanged. The second follows a thirty-unit cost saving through different price and income arrangements. The third returns to a workshop and two purchasing decisions to distinguish a changed parameter, a moving constraint and information an aggregate cannot retain. The sequence moves from recalculating an answer to identifying what a new account must preserve.
