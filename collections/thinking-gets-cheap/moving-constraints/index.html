Where the constraint moves

Where complements bind, who controls access and how long a premium lasts.

The next scarce thing can command attention without commanding all the income attributed to it. A necessary connection may be supplied competitively, reserved by contract or controlled exclusively. Each arrangement changes the bargain around the same physical contribution.

A claim on value depends on alternatives: who can reach another provider, how long expansion takes and which rights permit access. The ability to charge for a bottleneck is therefore an economic relationship to explain, not a property that follows automatically from its technical importance.

The horizon joins those questions. A high price can finance new supply, and new supply can remove the premium. A useful bridge need not remain a toll road.
