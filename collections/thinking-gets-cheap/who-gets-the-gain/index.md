Title: Who gets the gain

[Read HTML](https://post-agi-economics.org/collections/thinking-gets-cheap/who-gets-the-gain/)

A household may encounter cheaper services and less paid work at the same time. Whether it gains room to live depends on both movements, alongside its other income and commitments. The price of producing something does not alone determine the resources with which a person can buy it.

The connection runs through firms, contracts and institutions. Released time can support more output or a shorter working day; a saving can lower a price or increase an owner's income. A service can also [help a buyer resolve a purchase](https://post-agi-economics.org/collections/thinking-gets-cheap/who-gets-the-gain/when-the-buyer-finds-an-answer/) that would otherwise remain uncertain. Those arrangements shape demand and investment, influencing what happens next. Following the gain means understanding how useful work enters these relationships and how they carry it to particular people.
